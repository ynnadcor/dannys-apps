Engine Academy

Labs, mission briefings, quiz and field manual for designing an inline-8. Works offline.

Open

Option 1 · Home Screen (iPhone and iPad, no expiry)

  1. Open Engine Academy above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it once while online. After that it works offline.

Option 2 · SideStore or AltStore source

  1. In SideStore or AltStore, open Sources, tap +, paste the link.
  2. Open Danny's Apps and install Engine Academy.
  3. With a free Apple ID, refresh the app at least every 7 days. A free ID allows 3 sideloaded apps at once, SideStore included.
